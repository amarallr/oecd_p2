# OECD Handbook figures for Pillar Two

**Source S1 · 2023 edition · accessed 24 October 2026.** [Original PDF](https://www.oecd.org/content/dam/oecd/en/topics/policy-sub-issues/global-minimum-tax/minimum-tax-implementation-handbook-pillar-two.pdf) · [OECD terms and conditions](https://www.oecd.org/en/about/terms-conditions.html).

The handbook introduces the GloBE rules and implementation considerations. The publication itself says it does not modify the application or interpretation of the Model Rules, Commentary or Agreed Administrative Guidance. Treat it as an explanatory 2023 publication, not as a standalone source of current law.

The [bilingual visual guide](../../site/figuras-pilar-dois.html) contains original, summarized vector adaptations of all nine figures listed below. Concepts are translated into Brazilian Portuguese and US English. Figure 2.1 retains the published example values. The original images, OECD logo and visual identity are not reproduced.
